From the widget — sheet over the home screen, app never opens

Tapping a row in the Announcements widget opens this sheet directly — the full text, no truncated preview and no app launch. The dimmed home screen stays behind it, so dismissing drops you right back where you were.

Why a sheet, not a dialog: announcements run long. A centered dialog would either clip or become a tiny scroll box. A bottom sheet grows with the content, scrolls naturally, and has room for two actions.

Implementation: the widget tap fires actionStartActivity at a translucent TrampolineActivity that hosts a Material 3 ModalBottomSheet and finishes on dismiss. Same composable is reused in-app, so there's one implementation, not two.

4×4 + 2×2 — dense and glanceable together

Sizes are real widget cells, not artboards. The 4×4 shows five items and is the one you'd actually live with; the 2×2 pair is for a second home-screen page.

4×2 2 rows + “+N more” 4×3 3 rows 4×4 5 rows 2×2 3 compact lines

Tap behaviour: row opens detail, checkbox completes, ⋯ opens Snooze / Tomorrow / Dismiss. The ⋯ is a real button because widgets have no long-press.
